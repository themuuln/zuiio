# zuiio — Vision

## What we're building

zuiio is a family of software products — B2B platforms and consumer applications — built under one coherent brand. Business products cover ERP, POS, CRM, service management, gym/workout operations, and shop workflows for organizations. Consumer products include apps such as Tamga, Tumurch, and future lifestyle products. Every product shares the same design language, engineering standards, and philosophy, but ships independently and serves its own users.

## Why it exists

Most software is either too generic to be useful or too narrow to grow. We build products that are opinionated where it matters and flexible where it doesn't — tools that feel like they were made specifically for the person using them.

## How we build

**One codebase, many products.** Shared packages for UI, utilities, and logic. Each app is its own thing, but none of them start from zero.

**Design is not an afterthought.** Every product launches with a complete design system, consistent motion, and a voice. Consistency compounds — users who touch one zuiio product should feel at home in any other.

**Ship fast, maintain ruthlessly.** Prefer doing one thing well over ten things adequately. Remove features that don't earn their weight. Keep the codebase smaller than it wants to be.

**No half-built things in production.** An unfinished feature is worse than a missing one. Ship complete or don't ship.

## What we won't do

- Build things just because they're technically interesting
- Sacrifice consistency for speed
- Let the monorepo become a dumping ground
- Ship without considering the person on the other end
