# ZRAf — Mongolian Driving Theory Exam Prep

## Vision

Help Mongolian learners pass the traffic theory exam faster through interactive practice, mock exams, and progress tracking.

## Target Audience

- **Individual learners** preparing for the Mongolian driving theory exam
- **Driving school students** who want extra practice outside class

## Problem

The official Traffic Institute exam is intimidating. Learners need:
- Familiarity with question formats and traffic signs
- Practice under timed conditions
- Tracking of weak topics to focus study

## Key Differentiators

- **Offline-capable**: All data is static JSON, works without backend
- **Visual learning**: Traffic sign images alongside questions
- **Smart practice**: Weak-topic identification and targeted drills
- **Mongolian-first**: UI and explanations in Mongolian

## Success Criteria

- Deployed on Vercel, accessible via shareable link
- 1 tester can complete full practice flows (onboard → practice → results → progress)
- Question bank loads correctly with images
