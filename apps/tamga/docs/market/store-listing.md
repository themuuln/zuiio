## English Description (International Market — 4000 chars max)

Tamga is the all-in-one horse management app built for Mongolian horse owners, breeders, and equestrian professionals. Track everything about your horses — health, pedigree, documents, and marketplace — in one powerful offline-first mobile app.

Mongolia is home to over 4 million horses. Horse owners currently manage their animals across notebooks, WhatsApp groups, paper vet certificates, and memory. Tamga replaces all of that with a single digital platform.

**Key Features:**

**Horse Records** — Create a digital passport for each horse. Store name, age, color, brand mark (tamga), breed, photos, and ownership history. Every horse gets a permanent digital identity.

**Pedigree Tree** — Visualize 3–5 generation lineage trees with inbreeding coefficient calculation. The most detailed pedigree tool available in Mongolian, built specifically for breeders who care about bloodline purity.

**Health Tracking** — Log vaccinations, deworming treatments, vet visits, and medical records. Smart reminders ensure you never miss critical health events — the number one pain point for horse owners.

**Farrier Logs** — Track shoeing, trimming, and hoof treatment schedules. Set timed reminders so your horses always get proper hoof care.

**Document Vault** — Store vet certificates, insurance papers, registration documents, and microchip records in one secure place. Pull out your phone at competitions or vet clinics — everything is at your fingertips.

**Marketplace** — Buy, sell, or lease horses with verified pedigree data and ownership history. Trusted records reduce fraud and build confidence in every transaction.

**Breeding Tools** — Gestation calculators, breeding planners, and offspring tracking. Professional tools for serious breeders.

**Offline-First** — Full functionality without internet. Essential for rural Mongolia where connectivity is limited. Your data syncs automatically when you reconnect.

**Why Tamga?**
