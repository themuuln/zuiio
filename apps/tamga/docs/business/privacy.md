# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 2026
**Last Updated:** June 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Tamga LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use the Tamga mobile application ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with Mongolia's **Personal Data Protection Law** (Хувийн мэдээлэл хамгаалах тухай хууль, 2021 revision).

By using the App, you consent to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

| Data Type | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Account Information** | Name, email address, phone number | Account creation and authentication |
| **Horse Records** | Horse name, age, breed, color, brand mark, photos | Horse management and pedigree tracking |
| **Health Records** | Vaccination dates, vet visits, treatments, farrier logs | Health tracking and reminders |
| **Documents** | Vet certificates, insurance papers, registration documents | Document storage and retrieval |
| **Marketplace Listings** | Horse descriptions, prices, photos | Buying, selling, or leasing horses |
| **Communications** | Support requests, feedback | Customer support |

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

| Data Type | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Device Information** | Device model, OS version, app version | Compatibility and debugging |
| **Usage Data** | Features used, session duration, navigation patterns | Product improvement |
| **Crash Reports** | Error logs, stack traces | Stability and bug fixes |
| **Purchase History** | Subscription tier, payment dates | Subscription management |

### 1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

- **Location data** (GPS) — Not collected at this time. May be added in future versions with explicit consent.
- **Contacts** — We do not access your phone contacts.
- **Health data** — We do not collect personal human health data. All health records in the App are for horses.
- **Biometric data** — We do not collect fingerprints, face recognition, or other biometric identifiers.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Storage

Your data is stored using **Supabase**, a cloud database platform. Data is stored on Supabase servers located in regions selected for optimal performance and compliance.

### 3.2 Security Measures

| Measure | Description |
|---|---|
| **Encryption in Transit** | All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3 |
| **Encryption at Rest** | All stored data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption |
| **Access Control** | Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensure you can only access your own data |
| **Authentication** | Secure phone-based OTP authentication |
| **Regular Backups** | Automatic daily backups of all data |

### 3.3 Offline Data

When you use the App offline, your data is stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage (Isar). This data is synced to the cloud when an internet connection is available. Local data is protected by your device's security features (passcode, biometrics).

## 4. Data Sharing

### 4.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

### 4.2 Service Providers

We share data with the following service providers who assist in operating the App:

| Provider | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Cloud database and authentication | Account data, horse records, documents |
| **RevenueCat** | Subscription management and billing | Purchase history, device identifiers |
| **Firebase** | Push notifications, crash reporting | Device tokens, crash logs |
| **Apple** | iOS App Store | In-app purchase data |
| **Google** | Google Play Store | In-app purchase data |

All service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the purposes described above.

### 4.3 Marketplace

When you create a marketplace listing, certain information (horse details, photos, asking price) is visible to other App users who browse the marketplace. Your personal contact information is not shared until you choose to communicate with a potential buyer.

### 4.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Tamga LLC, our users, or the public.

## 5. Your Rights

Under Mongolia's Personal Data Protection Law, you have the following rights:

| Right | Description |
|---|---|
| **Right to Access** | You can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you |
| **Right to Correction** | You can request correction of inaccurate data |
| **Right to Deletion** | You can request deletion of your personal data |
| **Right to Restrict Processing** | You can request that we limit how we use your data |
| **Right to Data Portability** | You can request your data in a machine-readable format |
| **Right to Withdraw Consent** | You can withdraw consent for data processing at any time |

### How to Exercise Your Rights

- **In-App:** Go to Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account
- **Email:** Send a request to privacy@tamga.mn
- **Response Time:** We will respond to all requests within 30 days
