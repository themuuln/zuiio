# Shell — Main App Home (v2 — Editorial + Rich Cards)

Design a visually rich home screen for a Mongolian horse management app called Tamga.

Background: Warm white #FDF6F3.

Header:
- Top-left: "Сайн байна уу, Тэмүүлэн" in 28pt bold #2E2D2C
- Top-right: circular avatar (48dp, photo of a Mongolian man, border #D4CBC8)
- Below header row: a horizontal weather strip — small cloud/sun icon, "Улаанбаатар · 18°C" in 13pt #777171, rounded pill background #F1E6E2

Body (scrollable):
- Horse carousel: a horizontally scrollable row of 2 large horse cards. Each card is 280dp wide, 200dp tall, with a full-bleed landscape horse photo as background, dark gradient scrim at bottom 40%, rounded corners (16dp). On top of the photo: horse name in 20pt bold white at bottom-left, breed in 13pt white 80% opacity below name, and a small orange badge pill top-right ("Шарга азарга"). First card has a golden horse photo, second has a dark brown horse.

- Quick actions grid: 2x2 grid of square tiles below carousel with generous spacing. Each tile: 60dp circular icon (light warm background) centered, label below in 13pt #2E2D2C. Tiles: "Эрүүл мэнд" with stethoscope icon, "Бүртгэл" with document icon, "Газрын зураг" with map pin icon, "Баримт" with folder icon. Tiles have soft rounded corners, no shadows, background #F1E6E2.

- Section title "Сүүлийн үйлдлүүд" in 20pt semibold #2E2D2C with generous top spacing.

- Activity feed: 3 items stacked vertically. Each item has a small horse photo thumbnail (40dp circle) on left, title in 15pt #2E2D2C, timestamp in 12pt #777171 below, and a status badge on right (green pill "Баталгаатай" or orange pill "Хүлээгдэж байна"). Items separated by hairline dividers #D4CBC8.

Footer:
- Bottom tab bar: 5 tabs with icons and labels. Tabs: "Угшил" (home icon, filled orange), "Морьд" (horse outline, gray), "Уралдаан" (trophy outline, gray), "Газар зүй" (map outline, gray), "Зах" (bag outline, gray). White background, hairline top border #D4CBC8, labels in 10pt.

Style: ultra-minimal flat, warm earth tones, premium editorial feel. No shadows, no elevation, whitespace for hierarchy. The horse carousel is the visual hero — large photos create emotional connection. Mobile app (iPhone).
