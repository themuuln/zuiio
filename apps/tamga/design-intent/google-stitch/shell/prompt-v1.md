# Shell — Main App Home

Design the main home screen for a Mongolian horse management app called Tamga.

Background: Warm white #FDF6F3.

Header:
- Top-left: large greeting "Сайн байна уу" in 28pt bold #2E2D2C
- Below greeting: user name "Тэмүүлэн" in 17pt regular #777171
- Top-right: circular avatar placeholder (40dp, #F1E6E2)

Body:
- Stats row: 3 compact cards in a horizontal row, each with a large number on top and label below. First card: "4" / "Морь" (orange number). Second card: "12" / "Тэмдэглэл" (green number). Third card: "2" / "Уралдаан" (orange number). Cards have warm white background #F1E6E2, no shadows, softly rounded.
- Section title "Сүүлийн үйлдлүүд" in 20pt semibold #2E2D2C, below stats row with generous spacing.
- 2 list items below title: each has a small circular icon (warm background) on the left, title in 17pt #2E2D2C, subtitle in 15pt #777171, and a small chevron right. Items separated by hairline dividers #D4CBC8.

Footer:
- Bottom tab bar with 5 icons evenly spaced: Home (filled, orange), Horses (outline, gray), Lineage (outline, gray), Market (outline, gray), Profile (outline, gray). Tab bar has white background, hairline top border #D4CBC8.

Style: ultra-minimal, warm, organic. No shadows, no card elevation, whitespace for hierarchy. Mobile app (iPhone).
