# Нүүр — Daily Command Center

Design a home screen for a Mongolian horse management app called Tamga. This is the owner's daily triage board — what needs attention right now.

Background: Warm white #FDF6F3.

Header:
- Top-left: "Сайн байна уу, Тэмүүлэн" in 28pt bold #2E2D2C
- Top-right: circular avatar (photo, border #D4CBC8)
- Below: rounded pill with cloud icon + "Улаанбаатар · 18°C" in 13pt #777171, background #F1E6E2

Body (scrollable):

Herd health strip — a row of 5 small circular horse avatars. Three have thin green border rings (healthy), one has orange ring (vaccination due), one has red ring (needs attention). Horse names below each in 11pt #777171. The first horse is slightly larger.

Attention card — a full-width section with title "Анхаарах зүйлс" in 17pt semibold #2E2D2C and an orange circle with number "3" next to it. Below: 3 items stacked, each with a small colored dot (red or orange) on left, task description in 15pt #2E2D2C, and a small right arrow. Items describe which horse needs what and why. At bottom of card: "Бүгдийг харах" in orange 15pt.

Upcoming events — section title "Ирэх арга хэмжээ" in 20pt semibold #2E2D2C. Below: 2 cards side by side, each with event name in 15pt bold, date in 12pt #777171, and a location pin icon with city name.

Recent activity — section title "Сүүлийн үйлдлүүд" in 20pt semibold. Below: 2 items, each with small horse photo circle, activity text, timestamp, and a colored status badge pill (green or orange).

Footer:
- Tab bar: 5 icons with labels — "Нүүр" (home, filled orange), "Морьд" (horse, gray), "Арчилгаа" (calendar, gray), "Угшил" (tree, gray), "Зах" (bag, gray). White background, hairline top border.

Style: ultra-minimal flat, warm earth tones, premium. No shadows, no elevation. The health strip and attention card are the visual heroes. Mobile app (iPhone).
