# Зах — Marketplace & Ownership

Design a marketplace screen for a Mongolian horse management app called Tamga. This is where owners buy, sell, and transfer horses.

Background: Warm white #FDF6F3.

Header:
- Title "Зах зээл" in 28pt bold #2E2D2C
- Below: filter pills — "Бүгд" (selected, filled orange) / "Зарах" / "Үржүүлэх" / "Гэрчилгээ" in 15pt. Unselected pills have outline border.

Body (scrollable):

Featured listing — a full-width card at top with a large horse photo (landscape, 200dp tall), dark gradient scrim at bottom 30%, rounded 16dp. On the photo: horse name "Хүрэн азарга" in 20pt bold white, breed and age in 13pt white 80% below, and a green badge "Баталгаатай" top-right. Below photo: price "₮ 2,500,000" in 17pt bold #2E2D2C and location "Төв аймаг" in 12pt #777171 with a pin icon.

Listing grid — 2 columns of listing cards below featured. Each card: horse photo (square, rounded 12dp), name in 15pt bold #2E2D2C, breed in 12pt #777171, price in 15pt bold #2E2D2C, and a small heart icon top-right. Cards have white background, thin border #D4CBC8.

My listings section — section title "Миний зарууд" in 20pt semibold #2E2D2C. Below: 1 compact item with small horse photo circle, listing name in 15pt, status pill "Идэвхтэй" (green) or "Зарагдсан" (gray) on right.

Footer:
- Tab bar: 5 icons with labels — "Нүүр" (home, gray), "Морьд" (horse, gray), "Арчилгаа" (calendar, gray), "Угшил" (tree, gray), "Зах" (bag, filled orange). White background, hairline top border.

Style: ultra-minimal flat, warm earth tones, editorial. No shadows, no elevation. The featured listing is the visual hero — large photo creates emotional connection. Mobile app (iPhone).
