# Угшил — Lineage & Breeding

Design a lineage and breeding screen for a Mongolian horse management app called Tamga. This shows family trees and breeding status.

Background: Warm white #FDF6F3.

Header:
- Title "Угшил судалгаа" in 28pt bold #2E2D2C
- Below: 2 toggle pills — "Мод" (selected, filled orange) / "Үржүүлэг" in 15pt. Unselected has outline border.

Body (scrollable):

Family tree section — a visual pedigree tree showing 3 generations. Top row: 1 large horse card (the selected horse) with photo, name "Босоо хээр", breed "Монгол адуу" in 17pt bold white on a dark scrim over the photo, rounded 12dp. Below: 2 parent cards connected by thin lines, smaller size, each with photo and name. Below parents: 4 grandparent cards, even smaller, with names only. Tree lines are thin #D4CBC8. Background of tree area is slightly darker #F1E6E2.

Breeding status section — section title "Хээлтийн төлөв" in 20pt semibold #2E2D2C. Below: 2 status cards side by side. Each card: horse photo circle, horse name in 15pt bold, status in 13pt with colored dot — "Хээлтсэн · 120 хоног" with green dot, "Хугацаа: 7-р сарын 15" in 12pt #777171. Cards have white background, rounded 12dp, thin border #D4CBC8.

Inbreeding alert — a compact card with title "Цус ойртолт" in 15pt semibold, a circular gauge showing "2.3%" in orange, and text "Аюулгүй түвшин" in 12pt #777171 below. Background #F1E6E2, rounded 12dp.

Footer:
- Tab bar: 5 icons with labels — "Нүүр" (home, gray), "Морьд" (horse, gray), "Арчилгаа" (calendar, gray), "Угшил" (tree, filled orange), "Зах" (bag, gray). White background, hairline top border.

Style: ultra-minimal flat, warm earth tones, editorial. No shadows, no elevation. The family tree is the visual hero — lines and cards create hierarchy. Mobile app (iPhone).
