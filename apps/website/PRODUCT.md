# Product Context — zuiio.com

## Register

**brand** — design IS the product. This is the marketing site for Zuiio; a visitor's impression of the company is the deliverable.

## Target Users

- Mongolian SME owners and operators evaluating business software
- Auto repair shop owners looking for management tools
- Decision-makers who need to see pricing and product fit quickly
- Both Mongolian-speaking (primary) and English-speaking audiences

## Product Purpose

Communicate Zuiio's value proposition to Mongolian businesses and convert visitors into users. Two products under one brand:
- **Zer** — mini ERP (inventory, sales, expenses, customer management)
- **Tolom** — auto repair shop management (scheduling, work orders, estimates)

## Brand Personality

- **Practical** — no fluff, shows what the software does
- **Proud** — built for Mongolia, not adapted from elsewhere
- **Warm** — approachable, not corporate

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS cream/beige marketing pages
- Corporate enterprise software sites
- Adapted-from-English templates with Mongolian text pasted in
- Tech-startup-with-no-product landing pages

## Design Principles

1. Content-first: every section earns its space with real information
2. Mongolian-first: the site works in Cyrillic Mongolian before English
3. Honest marketing: show real features and pricing, no vaporware aesthetics

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Skip-to-content link present
- aria-labels on interactive elements
- Keyboard navigable
- Bilingual (mn/en) with locale-aware routing

## Fonts

- Display: Instrument Serif (headings)
- Body: Geist (sans-serif)
