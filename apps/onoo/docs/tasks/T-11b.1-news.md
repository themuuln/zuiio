# T-11b.1 — News articles (admin CRUD + public pages + home strip)

## Why

The site had no public news feature — only academy-scoped announcements visible on
the guardian portal. This task adds league-level news: admin-managed articles with
a draft/publish lifecycle, a public listing + detail page, and a latest-news strip
on the landing page.
