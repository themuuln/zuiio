## Goal

FG / 3P / FT percentages visualized as radial gauge rings, with a points breakdown bar showing the share of scoring from 2P / 3P / FT.

## Why

Shooting percentages are more intuitive as gauges than raw numbers — a half-filled ring immediately communicates "shoots 45% from the field." The points breakdown reveals shot selection and efficiency at a glance.

## Implementation Steps

1. **Data helper**: `getPlayerShootingProfile(playerId, competitionId)` in `src/app/lib/data.ts` — queries `v_public_leaders` for player's fgm, fga, tpm, tpa, ftm, fta. Returns typed object or `null`.
2. **`GaugeRing` sub-component**: single Recharts `RadialBarChart` with a filled arc from 225° to -45°, percentage centered. Shows `formatStat(value)` (e.g., "45.2%") and made/attempted caption below (e.g., "38/84").
3. **`PointsBreakdown` sub-component**: horizontal stacked bar computed — 2P pts = (fgm − tpm)×2, 3P pts = tpm×3, FT pts = ftm. Colors differentiate the three sources.
4. **`ShootingProfile` component**: three gauges side by side (FG%, 3P%, FT%) using CSS grid, responsive: single column at <sm, three columns at ≥sm. Below gauges, the points breakdown bar. Color uses `var(--team-color)` accent for gauges.
5. **Zero-attempt handling**: if `fga === 0` (no FG attempts), render em-dash (`—`) gauge instead of 0%. Same for `tpa` and `fta`.
6. **Lazy load**: import through `chart-sections.tsx` `dynamic()` wrapper.
7. **Integrate into player page**: render after season arc under "Шидэлт" section heading.

## Design Decisions

- **RadialBarChart for gauges**: Recharts' `RadialBarChart` with a single bar at 100% track width creates the ring effect. The track (unfilled portion) is rendered as a second `RadialBar` with muted color.
- **Gauge arc range**: 225° to -45° (270° sweep) — creates a "half-donut" with the opening at bottom-center, which looks cleaner than a full circle for percentage display.
- **Color usage**: gauge fill uses `var(--brand-sports)` (the app's sports accent color), track uses `var(--color-sidebar-accent)` (muted). Points breakdown uses distinct colors for 2P / 3P / FT with semantic basketball convention.
- **Em-dash for zero attempts**: showing 0% when a player hasn't attempted a three-pointer is misleading (they might be a center who never shoots threes). Em-dash communicates "not applicable."

## Risks & Gotchas

- **Gauge square vs circle**: Recharts `RadialBarChart` renders inside a square SVG. If the container is wider than tall, the gauge distorts. The component uses explicit `width`/`height` or `cx/cy` to maintain circular proportions.
- **Color contrast on gauge text**: percentage text overlaid on the gauge must be on a neutral (non-colored) background, not the filled arc, to maintain readability.
- **Division by zero**: `fga === 0` → skip percentage computation entirely, render em-dash. Same for tpa/fta. The data helper returns raw counts; the component handles zero-attempt display.
