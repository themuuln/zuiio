## Goal

Homepage answers "who plays tonight / what's the score / who won" at a glance.

## Why

The homepage is the first thing users see. It must surface live, upcoming, and recent games immediately so the site feels alive and useful as a daily hub for Mongolian basketball fans.

## Design Decisions

- Section order: live → today → yesterday → standings. Live is the scarcest AND most valuable — it goes first.
- Score center is a `div` with `text-center` and `tabular-nums` for stable alignment during number changes.
- Winner's score bolded using a simple comparison of final scores server-side before render.
- Polling is client-side only and leaves a `TODO(5.1)` for the realtime upgrade — avoids premature optimization.

## Risks & Gotchas

- Live games with provisional status: per 5.1 spec, they must appear but with "Урьдчилсан" badge. For now (pre-5.1), only `league_verified|official` games appear in the view. The wrapper should handle missing `getLiveGames` gracefully (empty array).
- date-fns-tz import path differs from date-fns — verify the exact import for `Asia/Ulaanbaatar` timezone formatting.
- Removing nothing that exists: audit current homepage to ensure no content loss.
