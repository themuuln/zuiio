## Goal

Roster grid of mini player cards, team identity throughout.

## Why

Team pages are where fans, scouts, and league organizers go to understand a team's composition at a glance. A visual roster grid with player mini-cards plus team identity header makes the team tangible and browseable.

## Data Dependencies

| Entity | Access | Purpose |
|--------|--------|---------|
| `getTeamBySlug(slug)` | Execute | Team details, logo, colors, record |

## Design Decisions

- Roster grid uses CSS Grid (`grid-template-columns: repeat(2, 1fr)` at 360px, `md:grid-cols-4`). No JS — pure responsive CSS.
- Mini card is a clickable `<Link>` wrapping a shadcn `Card` component (already available).
- Form guide dots use the same 6px dot pattern as standings last-10 (T-3.2) — reuse for visual consistency.
- Players without PPG get a dash `—` instead of "0.0" to distinguish "not qualified" from "averaging zero."

## Risks & Gotchas

- `v_public_team_roster` must exist. If it doesn't, create a view joining `team_players`, `players`, and `player_seasons`. Follow the naming convention of existing `v_public_*` views.
- Team season ID derivation: the slug corresponds to a team, but the roster is per `team_season`. Ensure the page fetches the correct season ID for the active competition.
- Position labels: Mongolian abbreviations (`PG`, `SG`, `SF`, `PF`, `C` or their Mongolian equivalents). Check what `players.position` stores.
- Form guide: filter `v_public_games` where the team is either home_team_id or away_team_id. Sort by date desc, limit 5.
