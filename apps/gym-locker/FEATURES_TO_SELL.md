# Features to Sell to Gym Owners

## Context

You already have:
- **Locker management** (QR check-in/out, offline-first)
- **Mini ERP** (products, inventory, POS)

This doc covers everything else a gym owner needs — ranked by revenue impact and pain relief.

---

## Pain Point #1: Member Churn (50% leave in year 1)

**The problem:** Most gyms bleed members silently. By the time the owner notices someone stopped coming, they're already gone.

**Features to build:**

| Feature | What it does | Revenue impact |
|---|---|---|
| **At-risk scoring** | Auto-calculates churn risk based on visit frequency, locker usage, and POS activity drop-offs. Flags members who haven't visited in 7/14/30 days. | Retaining 1 extra member/month at $50/mo = $600/yr saved acquisition cost |
| **Inactivity alerts** | Staff gets notified: "John hasn't checked in for 10 days — send a message." One-tap to send a friendly message via SMS or in-app. | Turns passive churn into active retention |
| **Onboarding journeys** | First 30 days: auto-sequence of welcome tasks, equipment orientation reminders, check-in milestones. Members who complete onboarding stay 3x longer. | Industry data: structured onboarding boosts retention 30-50% |
| **Check-in streaks** | Member app shows "You've visited 12 days in a row!" Gamification drives habit formation. | Streak users visit 40% more often |
| **Milestone automation** | Auto message on 10th, 50th, 100th visit: "Congrats on 100 visits! Here's a free guest pass." | Low effort, high member delight |

**Offline advantage:** Visit data is already local from locker check-ins. No internet needed to calculate risk scores — runs on the device.

## Pain Point #2: Cash Flow Leakage (failed payments, manual chasing)

**The problem:** 10-20% of recurring payments fail each month. Owners spend hours chasing payments via WhatsApp/in-person. Some just give up and write it off.

**Features to build:**

| Feature | What it does | Revenue impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Membership billing plans** | Create plans (monthly/annual/punch-card), auto-track dues, show who's paid and who hasn't. | Eliminates "I forgot" revenue loss |
| **Dunning / payment retry** | When payment fails: log it, retry after 3 days, send reminder. Track resolution. | Recovers 5-10% of monthly revenue |
| **Debt dashboard** | One screen: total outstanding, aging (7/30/60/90+ days), member contact info. Staff can settle at front desk. | Turns soft debt into collectible revenue |
| **Freeze/cancel workflows** | Member wants to freeze? Process it formally with start/end dates, auto-resume billing. No more verbal agreements. | Prevents free "I'll pay later" limbo |
| **Cash reconciliation** | End-of-day: match POS takings against expected revenue. Flag discrepancies. | Reduces theft and errors |

**Offline advantage:** Staff can record payments, check dues, and sell memberships even when internet is down. All syncs when back online.

## Pain Point #3: Leads & New Member Growth

**The problem:** Walk-ins and trial visitors pass through every day. Without a system, most are forgotten. Owners have no idea which marketing works.

**Features to build:**

| Feature | What it does | Revenue impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead CRM** | Capture name + phone on walk-in. Track lead source (walk-in, Instagram, referral, ad). Status tracking: new → toured → trial → member → lost. | Converts 20% more leads by systematizing follow-up |
| **Trial pass management** | Issue 1-day/3-day/1-week trial passes with expiry. Track conversion rate. Auto-reminder on last day of trial. | Typical conversion from trial: 15-25%. Systematizing can push to 30%+. |
| **Referral tracking** | Existing member sends a referral link/QR. When friend signs up, both get a reward (free week, discount). | Referral members have highest retention and LTV |
| **Win-back campaigns** | Auto-trigger for expired members: "It's been 3 months, come back for a free week." Track re-activation rate. | Re-activation costs 5x less than new acquisition |
| **Abandoned trial follow-up** | If trial pass was issued but never activated/used, auto-reminder after 2 days. | Recovers trials that would otherwise go unused |

## Pain Point #4: Staff Chaos (scheduling, payroll, accountability)

**The problem:** Staff calls in sick. Shift coverage is a group chat nightmare. Trainers under-report hours. No one knows who's supposed to clean what.

**Features to build:**

| Feature | What it does | Revenue impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Staff shift scheduling** | Weekly schedule with drag-and-drop. Staff see their shifts. Swap/request coverage in-app. | Saves owner 2-3 hrs/week on scheduling |
| **Clock in/out** | Staff taps "clock in" on the reception app when they arrive. Tracks actual hours vs scheduled. | Prevents "I forgot to log my extra hour" disputes. Saves 5-10% on payroll fraud. |
| **Trainer commission tracking** | Log sessions per trainer. Auto-calc commission (per session or %). Staff can see their earnings. | Motivates trainers to upsell and retain clients |
| **Task checklists** | Daily/opening/closing checklists on the tablet: "Wipe down all machines? ✓ Restock towels? ✓ Locker room check? ✓" Owner can verify remotely. | Ensures consistent facility quality |
| **Class coverage alerts** | If trainer marks sick, auto-notify backup trainers on roster. Find replacement in minutes. | Prevents last-minute class cancellations that annoy members |

**Offline advantage:** Clock in/out and task checklists work even when internet is unreliable. No excuses.

## Pain Point #5: Class & Trainer Utilization

**The problem:** Classes run with 3 people in a room that fits 20. No-shows waste slots. Trainers have empty hours. Owner doesn't know which classes are profitable.

**Features to build:**

| Feature | What it does | Revenue impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Class booking** | Members book slots in-app or at reception. Auto-capacity limit. Waitlist with auto-promotion. | Full classes = max revenue per hour |
| **No-show policy** | Track no-shows. Auto-charge a fee or deduct from punch-card after 3 no-shows in a month. | Reduces no-shows by 30-50% |
| **Capacity heatmap** | Visual grid: Mon 6pm = full, Wed 10am = empty. Owner adjusts schedule based on data. | Optimizes schedule for revenue, not guesswork |
| **Trainer utilization report** | % of paid hours with a client vs waiting. Identify which trainers are under-performing. | Drive more PT revenue per trainer |
| **Class pack management** | Members buy 10-class packs. Auto-deduct on attendance. Show remaining balance. | Prepaid revenue is best revenue |

## Pain Point #6: Facility Maintenance & Cleanliness

**The problem:** A broken treadmill stays broken for 2 weeks because no one reported it properly. Members get annoyed and leave. Cleaning staff miss spots.

**Features to build:**

| Feature | What it does | Revenue impact |
|---|---|---|
| **QR issue reporting** | QR code on each machine/mirror/area. Member or staff scans → taps "Dumbbell rack loose" → creates maintenance ticket. Staff gets notified. | Fixes issues 3-5x faster. Members feel heard. |
| **Maintenance tickets** | Status tracking: reported → assigned → fixed → verified. Time-to-fix metrics. Parts used tracking. | Reduces equipment downtime. Protects equipment investment. |
| **Cleaning checklists** | Per-zone checklists for cleaning staff. Sign-off with photo. Time-stamped. Owner reviews. | Consistent cleanliness. Accountability for cleaning staff. |
| **Consumable stock alerts** | Towels running low? Soap dispenser empty? Staff reports via app. Auto-track reorder level. | No more "sorry, no towels" member complaints |

**Offline advantage:** QR on a machine works with any phone camera. Issue reported even in basement/back areas with no signal.

**Features to build:**

| Feature | What it does | Revenue impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner cockpit dashboard** | Single screen: active members, today's check-ins, today's revenue, outstanding dues, churn risk count, low stock alerts. Updated in real-time. | Saves owner 5+ hours/week of mental overhead |
| **Daily digest** | End-of-day push notification to owner: "37 check-ins, SAR 850 revenue, 3 unpaid invoices, 1 at-risk member, 2 low-stock items." | Owner knows business health in 10 seconds |
| **Weekly report PDF** | Auto-generated PDF: week-over-week attendance, revenue, new members, churn rate, top-selling products. | Basis for data-driven decisions instead of gut feel |
| **Locker usage heatmap** | Which lockers are most used? Peak hours? Under-utilized zones? Adjust pricing and layout. | Optimizes locker zone allocation for revenue |

**Offline advantage:** Since all data is local, the dashboard is instant. No loading spinners.

**The problem:** Members treat the gym as a transaction. No emotional connection → easy to cancel. Community-driven gyms have much higher retention.

**Features to build:**

## Key Insight: The Offline-First Advantage

Most gym management software competitors (Mindbody, Zenoti, GymMaster, PushPress) are **cloud-first** — they require internet for the core flow. This means:

- When internet is slow, scanning a QR code takes 3-5 seconds
- When internet is down, the gym cannot check members in or sell products
- Monthly fees add up (SAR 500-2000/mo for small gyms)
- Data is on someone else's server

Your system: **zero internet required, sub-second response, one-time cost, all data local.** For a small gym in a building with concrete walls and spotty WiFi, this is the difference between a usable system and a frustrating one.
