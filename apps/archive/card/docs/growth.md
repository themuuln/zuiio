# Growth & Go-To-Market (Adult Party Game Focus)

## Core Growth Loop

```
Guest joins a friend's Friday night game
  └── Has fun, zero friction to join
        └── Next Friday, THEY become the host
              └── Invite their own friends
                    └── Those friends become hosts too
```

Every guest is a potential future host. The product grows itself if the guest experience is delightful.

**This is why guests must never hit a paywall or signup wall.** The Spicy tier is enough for a full night of fun — After Dark is the upgrade.

---

## Viral Moment: The QR Code at a Party

The single highest-conversion channel: **one person opens the app, holds up their phone with the QR code, and 6 friends scan it in 10 seconds.** The public nature of this moment (everyone scans together, at a party, with drinks) makes it naturally viral.

**After Dark virality**: When a Wild or After Dark card gets a big reaction (gasps, laughter, "OMG"), the host says "it's the app!" → everyone wants to download.

## Launch Strategy

### Phase 0: Friends & Family (Now)
- Use it at your own Friday nights with the party game modes
- Target friend groups in their 20s–30s who drink socially
- Note which cards get the biggest reactions → those inform content priorities
- Target: 5 groups, 30 total players

### Phase 1: Organic (Month 1–3)
- **Reddit**: r/DrinkingGames, r/AskReddit (party game threads), r/cardsagainsthumanity, r/partyplanning
- **Twitter/X**: "Built an app because my friends kept asking 'got any cards?' on Friday nights. Now it has Truth or Dare and drinking games."
- **TikTok**: "The app that ruined my Friday night (in a good way)" — show the QR code moment + a spicy card reaction
- **Product Hunt**: Launch with the tagline "Your Friday nights just got spicier"
- Target: 500 MAU

### Phase 2: Content & Community (Month 3–6)
- **SEO blog**: "Best Truth or Dare questions for adults", "Never Have I Ever: the spiciest edition", "Drinking games for your next party"
- **User-generated content**: Encourage groups to share their funniest card moments (screenshot of a hot take)
- **Partner with dating/relationship influencers**: Adult party games are content gold for couples' channels
- **Seasonal hooks**: "Bachelorette party games" (wedding season), "NYE party games" (Dec), "Summer BBQ games" (July)
- Target: 5,000 MAU

### Phase 3: Partnerships & Events (Month 6–12)
- **College partnerships**: Student organizations, fraternities/sororities
- **Bar game nights**: "Download Friday for free drink specials"
- **Airbnb Experiences**: Hosts can offer "Friday Night Party Game" as an experience
- **Bachelorette/Bachelor party market**: SEO + Pinterest for wedding content
- Target: 25,000 MAU

## Virality Mechanics

### Built into the Product
- **The QR code** — every game night is a natural "what's that?" moment for first-timers
- **After Dark unlock** — if the host has After Dark, guests see "🔥 [Player] has After Dark content" — FOMO drives conversion
- **"Most Controversial Take" recap** — shareable card at the end of Hot Takes sessions
- **Session recap** — "Here's what happened in CREW" with winner, most drinks, most controversial vote
- **Room code as identity** — "Meet us at B O N D" is memorable at a party

### Referral (v2)
- Host shares a link → new guests who upgrade to Wild Pass get 1 month of Premium free
- Host gets 1 After Dark pack free for each new Premium subscriber they refer
- Group plan: any member can host with shared premium benefits

## Retention Strategy

### Weekly Habit Formation
- "Friday tonight?" push notification (opt-in, 5pm every Friday)
- "New cards added" notifications — content freshness drives replay
- After Dark pack release announcements (quarterly drops)
- Streak tracking: "You've played 6 Fridays in a row 🔥"

### Between Sessions
- Weekly: "Card of the week" — a single spicy question to answer solo
- Monthly: "Your group's stats" — who answered the most truths, who did the wildest dare
- "What's new" — tease upcoming After Dark packs and game modes

## Metrics That Matter (Adult Focus)

| Metric | Target | Why |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Time to first game | < 60s | Core product promise |
| Guest-to-host conversion | > 15% | Drives the growth loop |
| Week-2 retention | > 40% | Is it habit-forming? |
| Session length | 45–90 min | Optimal party length |
| Games per session | 3+ | Party game variety |
| Drinking engine usage | > 60% of sessions | Valued feature signal |
| After Dark conversion | > 15% of active users | Primary revenue driver |
| Content flag rate | < 1% | Safety systems working |
| Friday % of sessions | > 50% | Brand alignment |
| NPS | > 50 | Would you recommend? |

## Positioning

**Not**: A digital board game platform (too broad)
**Not**: A teen/kids' party game (wrong audience)
**Not**: A single-mode app like most Truth or Dare apps (too limited)
**Is**: The app you pull out at a party when drinks are flowing and someone says "let's play something spicy"

Competitors position on being a game platform or having clean family fun. Friday positions on **adult party content + zero friction for the moment you're already in.**
