# Friday — Adult Party Game Night App

> "Your Friday nights just got spicier."

## What It Is

A zero-friction **18+ party game app** for Friday nights with friends. The core problem: it's 9pm on Friday, everyone's together, drinks are flowing, and someone says "let's play something." Nobody brought cards, everyone has a phone.

Friday turns any group of phones into an unforgettable party night — spicy questions, daring challenges, drinking games, and classic card games, all with zero setup.

**This is NOT a teen game.** Every game mode is designed for adults 18+: revealing truths, spicy dares, drinking mechanics, and no-filter humor.

## The 60-Second Rule

From "let's play something" → first question/card dealt in under 60 seconds.

1. Host opens app → room created instantly
2. Room shows a QR code + 4-letter code (e.g. `FROG`)
3. Guests scan QR or visit `friday.app/FROG` — no install required
4. Everyone's in. Pick a game mode. The night begins.

## Who It's For

- Adult friend groups (4–20 people) who hang out on weekends
- Friday night drink-ups, pre-games, house parties, and casual gatherings
- People who want spicy party games but don't own physical card decks
- Mixed remote/in-person groups (some on couch, some on video)
