# Pricing & Monetization

## Philosophy

- **Joining is always free** — guests never pay, never sign up
- **Friction-free entry** is the product's core value — never paywalling the join flow
- Monetize the host, not the guest
- No ads during gameplay — ruins the vibe and kills retention
- **Spicy-tier content is free** — enough to have fun without paying
- **After Dark is the primary revenue driver** — people pay for explicit content

## Revenue Model (Adult-Focused)

### Conversion Funnel (Expected)
```
Free users (Spicy tier)
  └── 20% convert to Wild Pass ($2.99 one-time)
        └── 30% of Wild users buy After Dark packs ($2.99 each)
              └── 15% convert to Premium ($4.99/mo)
                    └── 10% of Premium → Group Plan ($9.99/mo)
```

### Year 1 Targets
- 5,000 MAU hosts
- Wild Pass: 1,000 purchases × $2.99 = ~$3,000
- After Dark packs: 500 purchases × $2.99 = ~$1,500
- Premium subs: 375 × $4.99/mo = ~$1,870/mo
- Group plans: 38 × $9.99/mo = ~$380/mo
- **Total MRR target (EoY)**: ~$5,250
- **Total first-year revenue**: ~$42,000

### Year 2 Targets
- 25,000 MAU hosts
- **Total MRR target**: ~$25,000

## What's Never Monetized

- Joining a room
- Viewing the scoreboard
- Reading the rules
- Basic UNO gameplay
- Spicy-tier content (enough to have a fun night)
- The core "scan and play" loop

The product grows by word of mouth at parties — if the host has After Dark, guests want it too.

---

## App Store Considerations

- iOS: Apple takes 30% (15% after year 1 under Small Business Program)
- Android: Google takes 15% for first $1M
- Web subscriptions via Stripe: 0% platform cut (just Stripe's ~2.9% + $0.30)
- After Dark packs sold as IAP or web purchase
- Note: Apple requires in-app purchase option to be available (can't only offer web)
