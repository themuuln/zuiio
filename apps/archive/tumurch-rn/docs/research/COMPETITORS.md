### Careby's critical weakness

**Careby added AI coaching but has no workout logger.** This is like building a GPS without a car. The AI generates a plan, but the member has nowhere to actually log their sets, reps, and weights. The plan lives in the AI, not in the member's hands.

**Tumurch's advantage:** We have BOTH — a Hevy-grade workout logger AND AI coaching. When our AI generates a plan, the member can immediately execute it in the app, track every set, see their progress, and get feedback. Careby's AI plan is a PDF; ours is a live, trackable, coachable workflow.

| **Tumurch** | **✅** | **✅** | **✅** | **✅** | **✅** |

**Tumurch is the only product that combines all five.** No competitor — local or global — does this.

### Careby's AI is a surface feature

Careby added AI coaching in v3.0.0 (April 2026), but it's a **plan generator without a logger**. The AI creates a workout plan, but the member has no way to:
- Log the actual sets, reps, and weights
- Track progress over time
- See PRs and body measurements
- Get real-time feedback during the workout
- Communicate with a human coach

**Our AI should be deeper:** not just "here's a plan" but "here's what you did last time, here's what to do today, here's how you're progressing, here's what your coach thinks."

### GymHub's gamification is a retention trick

GymHub uses XP, leaderboards, and leagues to keep users engaged. This is clever but superficial — it doesn't help gym owners run their business. We can adopt gamification elements (streaks, achievements) without building a social network.

## Zer integration plan

Tumurch's operations layer (membership, billing, shop, inventory) should share data with Zer (the ERP app). This creates a unified business platform.

### Integration points

| Tumurch feature | Zer module | Data flow |
|---|---|---|
| Membership plans | Zer Products/Services | Plans created in Zer, synced to Tumurch |
| Membership payments | Zer Invoices/Payments | Payments processed in Zer, reflected in Tumurch |
| Supplement shop | Zer POS/Inventory | Products, stock, orders flow between apps |
| Staff management | Zer HR | Staff roles, permissions, schedules |
| Revenue reporting | Zer Analytics | Tumurch sales data feeds Zer dashboards |
| Multi-branch | Zer Multi-branch | Branch settings, cross-branch inventory |

### Architecture

```
┌─────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐
│   Tumurch    │     │     Zer     │
│  (Mobile)    │     │   (Web)     │
├─────────────┤     ├─────────────┤
│ Workout log  │     │ ERP/POS     │
│ Member app   │     │ HR/Staff    │
│ Coach tools  │     │ Inventory   │
│ Reception    │     │ Analytics   │
└──────┬──────┘     └──────┬──────┘
       │                    │
       └────────┬───────────┘
                │
         ┌──────┴──────┐
         │   Supabase   │
         │  (Shared DB) │
         └─────────────┘
```

Both apps share the same Supabase backend. Tumurch handles mobile-first member/coach/reception workflows. Zer handles web-first ERP/POS/HR/analytics. They share the same data through Supabase Realtime.
